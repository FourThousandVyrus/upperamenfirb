UARB Digital Platform Elevation

End-to-End Project Delivery & Shipment Documentation

Project Overview

The UARB website transformation project was executed as a complete institutional digital elevation initiative focused on rebuilding the brand experience from a static corporate presence into a modern, editorial-grade financial platform.

The objective was not only visual improvement, but also:

Establishing stronger institutional trust

Creating a modern Ghanaian identity online

Improving readability and usability across devices

Enhancing storytelling through imagery and motion

Structuring scalable frontend architecture for future growth

Preparing the platform for long-term maintainability

This document captures the complete project lifecycle from planning to deployment shipment.

1. Discovery & Brand Direction

Initial Assessment

The original platform had several limitations:

Traditional banking layouts lacking emotional connection

Minimal storytelling structure

Inconsistent visual hierarchy

Weak mobile presentation

Strategic Direction

The redesign direction focused on creating:

Human-centered storytelling

Clean scalability for future pages and services
